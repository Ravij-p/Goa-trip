Ravij’s Goa drive

    Times use today’s clock against the plan for the day you pick, so you can rehearse before Nov 13. Arrival is detected within 250 m of a stop. Routes and ETAs come from OpenStreetMap road data (OSRM), with 10% added for ghat and traffic slowdowns. If routing can’t load, it falls back to a speed-based estimate. Stop coordinates are approximate; tap “I’m here” if the app misses one. Stop pins are snapped to the nearest road.
